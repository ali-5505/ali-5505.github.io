AI Workout Performance Analyzer

Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn

A machine learning pipeline that predicts next-session workout performance from training load, sleep, and recovery metrics. Engineers rolling averages and trend-delta features, trains and compares Ridge Regression and Random Forest models, and evaluates them with cross-validation and diagnostic plots instead of trusting a single metric.
